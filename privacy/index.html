Privacy Statement

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This privacy statement explains what personal data is processed when you visit roaldnefs.com, why, and what your rights are. The short version: this site doesn’t use analytics, tracking, or advertising, and I don’t collect personal data myself.

Who is responsible

This website is run by Roald Nefs, based in the Netherlands, who is the controller for the processing described here. You can reach me at info@roaldnefs.com. If you’d like to encrypt your message, my PGP key is available.

What data is processed

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit a page, your browser sends a request to GitHub’s servers. As part of delivering the page and protecting the service, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser’s user agent. I don’t have access to these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for how GitHub handles this data.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in delivering this website securely and reliably.

Fonts

The fonts on this site are served from this website itself. No request is made to a third-party font service.

Email

If you email me, I use your email address and the contents of your message only to reply to you. I don’t share it with anyone else, and I delete it when it’s no longer needed for our conversation.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in responding to your message.

Browser storage

This site doesn’t store anything in your browser. See the cookie statement for details.

International transfers

GitHub is based in the United States, so the data described above may be processed outside the European Economic Area. GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Mastodon, and the sites of the companies I work for. Their own privacy policies apply once you follow those links.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, email info@roaldnefs.com. For data processed by GitHub, you can also contact GitHub directly.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Netherlands, that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

I may update this statement when the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
