# Privacy Statement

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

This privacy statement explains what personal data is processed when you visit roaldnefs.com, why, and what your rights are. The short version: this site uses Google Analytics, which only sets cookies if you agree to it, has no advertising, and the only other personal data collected is what you send in a booking request or an email.

## Who is responsible

This website is run by Roald Nefs, based in the Netherlands, who is the controller for the processing described here. You can reach me at [info@roaldnefs.com](mailto:info@roaldnefs.com). If you'd like to encrypt your message, my [PGP key](/key.asc) is available.

## What data is processed

### Hosting

This site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit a page, your browser sends a request to GitHub's servers. As part of delivering the page and protecting the service, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser's user agent. I don't have access to these logs. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for how GitHub handles this data.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in delivering this website securely and reliably.

### Analytics

This site uses Google Tag Manager, which runs Google Analytics 4, to see which pages are read and how visitors find them. The script is loaded from Google on every visit, so Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request.

Until you accept analytics in the cookie banner, Google Analytics runs without cookies. It doesn't set or read any, and it only sends Google signals that a page was viewed, without an identifier that recognises you on a later page or visit.

If you accept, Google Analytics collects the pages you view, the website you came from, your approximate location, your browser and device type, and an identifier kept in a cookie so a return visit can be recognised. Google Analytics 4 uses your IP address to work out the approximate location and doesn't store it. I only see the results as statistics, not as data about individual visitors.

Google Ireland Limited provides both services and processes this data on my behalf. Data linked to the cookie identifier is deleted after 14 months at most. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google handles data.

The legal basis for the cookies and the data linked to them is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). For loading the script and the cookieless signals it is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in knowing which pages are read. You can withdraw your consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page, which also removes the cookies.

### Fonts

The fonts on this site are served from this website itself. No request is made to a third-party font service.

### Email

If you email me, I use your email address and the contents of your message only to reply to you. I don't share it with anyone else, and I delete it when it's no longer needed for our conversation.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in responding to your message.

### Booking form

The form on the [training page](/training/#book-a-session) sends what you fill in to the [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/) account of [Warpnet](https://warpnet.nl), the company I work for: your name, email address, organisation, and the details of the session you have in mind, together with the address of the page the form is on. Nothing is sent until you submit the form, no HubSpot script is loaded, and the form sets no cookies.

Warpnet receives your request as a lead, and it is used to reply to you and to arrange the session. HubSpot stores this data in its data centre in the European Union and processes it on Warpnet's behalf. See the [HubSpot Privacy Policy](https://legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy) for how HubSpot handles data.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in responding to your request.

### Videos

The [videos page](/videos/) shows thumbnails that are served from this website itself. Nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play on a video. When you do, the player is loaded from YouTube, a service of Google, in its privacy-enhanced mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`). Google then receives your IP address and browser details, and YouTube may keep data in your browser to play the video. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google handles data.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in showing you the video you asked to play.

### Browser storage

This site stores your answer to the cookie banner in your browser and, if you accept analytics, the Google Analytics cookies. See the [cookie statement](/cookies/) for details.

## International transfers

GitHub, Google, and HubSpot are based in the United States, so the data described above may be processed outside the European Economic Area. All three participate in the [EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/).

## Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Mastodon, and the sites of the companies I work for. Their own privacy policies apply once you follow those links.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, email [info@roaldnefs.com](mailto:info@roaldnefs.com). For data processed by GitHub, Google, or HubSpot, you can also contact them directly, and for a booking request you can also contact Warpnet.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Netherlands, that is the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/).

## Changes

I may update this statement when the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
